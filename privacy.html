Privacy Policy — Incident Triage Copilot

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Vendor: RichCommunity Studio

Incident Triage Copilot is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira. It reads incident issues on the site where it is installed and drafts a triage suggestion (priority, confidence, reasoning, similar past incidents, suggested assignees) for a human to review and approve. Nothing is applied without human approval.

What data the app accesses

Only Jira data on the installing site, and only to compute triage suggestions:

  • Issue summaries, descriptions, status, priority, and project history.
  • Assignee and reporter references (display name and Atlassian account ID) of the issue and of similar past issues.
  • Past resolved issues in the same project, to find similar incidents.

What data the app stores

Triage drafts are stored in Atlassian Forge storage on the installing site until a human approves or applies them, or until a newer draft replaces them. A stored draft contains the suggested priority, confidence score, reasoning, similar past issues, and suggested assignees (display name and Atlassian account ID). Drafts are not deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Drafts are visible only to Jira users who already have permission to view the issue. The app keeps no copy of your data anywhere else.

What data the app shares

None. The app sends no data to any third-party service, runs no analytics or tracking, shows no advertising, and never sells data.
